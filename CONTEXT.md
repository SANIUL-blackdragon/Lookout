# Visual Beauty Archive

A local library of visually striking websites. It stores full interactive copies plus notes on why each one works.

## Language

**Library**:
The site in this repo. It lists Entries and replays their Captures.
_Avoid_: site, app, archive (as a noun for the whole thing)

**Entry**:
One archived website and its record. Title, source URL, credits, review, metadata.
_Avoid_: site, item, page

**Capture**:
One saved artifact of an Entry. Single-file HTML, screenshot, video, WACZ.
_Avoid_: save, snapshot, file

**Package**:
One uploadable bundle for an Entry. A ZIP with entry.json plus its Captures.
_Avoid_: bundle, upload, pack
